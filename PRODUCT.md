# ZED Park Management Dashboard

## Product
Operations and management dashboard for ZED Park, an evening-oriented leisure park
(Ferris wheel, rides, food & retail tenants, events, landscaped gardens). Read-only
monitoring of visitors, revenue, rides, guest experience, safety, utilities, and
landscape & lighting. All figures are demo data until real systems are connected.

## Users
- Park operations / facility managers: dense, scannable panels, alerts and drill-downs.
- Executives / owners: headline KPIs vs targets first, detail one click away.

## Brand commitments
- Green accents; premium feel; "Evening garden" world (dark default from the park's
  night scene, soft botanical light theme). Theme persisted per viewer.
- Currency EGP. Missing readings show "Not connected". Demo data is always labelled.
- Equipment monitoring is read-only by default; no control commands.

## Assumptions (inferred from the brief, not confirmed)
- Park opening hours 10:00–24:00; peak 18:00–22:00.
- Zones: Entrance Plaza, Ferris Wheel Plaza, Family Rides, Thrill Rides, Food Court,
  Retail Promenade, Central Garden, Events Lawn.
